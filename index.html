01About

I'm a Technical Consultant at Gios Technology, where I spend my days brainstorming with the Elastic Stack — migrating clusters, building Kibana dashboards, and keeping observability pipelines honest and unstuck. That's the world I am thriving in.

Alongside it, I'm building a second set of skills in the Microsoft ecosystem: Power BI, Power Query, and most recently Copilot Studio, where I completed Microsoft's training on building agents. The worlds are different, but eventually it really is about the same - taking messy, scattered information and giving it structure people can act upon.

Outside of both, I write and weave stories for my imagination. Custom poems and song lyrics too for people who want to say something they can't quite put into words themselves - that's a separate thread of my life, further down.
